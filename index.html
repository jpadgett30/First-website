Why should you even learn music?

Because it's fun, it's interesting, and has been found to have some mental health benefits as well as potential overall intelligence benefits.

According to GuitarChalk.dot, shcools with music programs on average have about a 19% higher graduation rate and almost 6% higher attendence.
While there probably are more factors at play and nothing exists in a vacuum and it could be said that if a school can afford a music program that it can probably also afford more help to its students to help them graduate, but I'd still like to think that learning music is just beneficial.

Is music for everyone?

No. Like everything, somethings are just not for some people, and playing music just isn't for some people. Despite that, I believe that anyone can learn, and with enough effor and practice anyone can learn and become good. So I encourage you to learn, Musical Education can be a great resource, but more than that, seek out other sources of Musical Education. This site isn't all encompassing, nowhere is.

I believe that Musical Education is very important, and if you care about ME, then I care about you.
